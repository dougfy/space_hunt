Getting Started

You begin docked at your home star. Build infrastructure, collect resources, and expand.

First 5 Minutes

1. Build a Mine — start producing Ore
2. Undock — tap UNDOCK to fly around
3. Collect pods — fly through glowing pods for fuel & resources
4. Explore — fly near a planet and tap SCAN
5. Build more — Solar Array for Energy, Habitat for Food

Resources

Ore — from Mines, exploration, pods
Food — from Habitats, exploration, pods
Energy — from Solar Arrays, exploration, pods
Fuel — from Refineries, pods. Required for travel

Navigating the Tiers

Galaxy → System → Planet → Belt. Fly past the edge of a view to zoom out a level.
System (solar) — your star and its planets. Fly into a planet's dashed ring to drop to Planet view.
Belt — the dotted asteroid ring. Fly onto the ring to enter and mine drifting rocks.
The ⊕ button shows entry rings and boundaries so you can see exactly where each zone starts.
In Galaxy view, ◄ SYSTEM always returns you to your current star.

Comms

PUBLIC reads the Reddit thread. PRIVATE holds direct messages from commanders.
ALLIANCE is your team's channel once you join one. BOARD ranks commanders by power.
Read first. You never need to send a message to finish a tutorial.

Colonization

1. Build a Colony Ship at your Dock
2. Fly to an unclaimed star in Galaxy view
3. Dock at the station and colonize

Combat

Build Shields and Cannons to defend your stars
Build Raiders to attack other players
Upgrade your ship: Scout → Destroyer → Frigate → Battleship

Controls

Click — move to target
WASD / Arrows / hjkl — rotate & thrust
Space — fire
Right-click — cancel target

Navigation

Fly near a star to enter its system
Fly near a belt ring to explore it
Collect fuel pods to keep moving

Economy

Each star has unique resource richness (1-10)
Home stars are guaranteed richness 5-10
Building output scales with star richness
