# Valcordia Space — Player Guide

Welcome to **Valcordia Space**, a multiplayer space exploration and strategy game that lives inside a Reddit post. Build your empire, explore a galaxy of 100+ stars, manage fleets, and compete with other players — all without leaving Reddit.

---

## Getting Started

When you first open the game, you'll see an asteroid belt mini-game. Click **Join** to start playing.

You begin **docked** at your home star's station with a single Scout ship. This is your base of operations — the star where you'll build your first infrastructure and launch your exploration of the galaxy.

### Your First 5 Minutes

1. **Look around** — You're docked at a station. The bottom panel shows your resources (Ore, Food, Energy) and available buildings.
2. **Build a Mine** — Tap the Mine building to start producing Ore. This is your first income source.
3. **Undock** — Tap away from the station to undock and fly around your star system.
4. **Collect fuel pods** — Glowing pods float in asteroid belts. Fly through them to collect fuel and resources.
5. **Explore a planet** — Fly near a planet to enter its surface. You might discover resources or artifacts!

## Controls

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Move to target | **Click/tap** where you want to go |
| Rotate & thrust | **WASD**, **Arrow keys**, or **hjkl** |
| Fire weapons | **Space bar** |
| Cancel target | **Right-click** |
| Zoom in/out | **Scroll wheel** or **pinch** |

The game auto-detects your input method. Click once to set a destination and your ship will navigate there automatically.

## Navigation — The Galaxy Map

The game uses a hierarchical zoom system. Zoom out far enough and you'll see the full galaxy. Zoom in and you'll enter individual star systems.

### Tiers

| View | What You See | How to Get There |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Galaxy** | All stars connected by jump links | Zoom all the way out |
| **System** | Planets, belts, orbital rings around a star | Click a star or zoom in |
| **Planet** | Station, asteroids, fuel pods, docking | Fly near a planet |

**Moving between stars:** In the Galaxy view, click a connected star to fly to it. Your ship will follow the jump link. This costs fuel.

**Tip:** Stars glow different colors based on who owns them. Your stars are green. Unclaimed stars are dim. Other players' stars show their color.

## Building Your Empire

### Resources

Everything runs on three resources:

| Resource | Source | Icon |
|----------|--------|------|
| **Ore** | Mines, exploration, asteroid pods | ⛏️ |
| **Food** | Habitats, exploration, pods | 🌾 |
| **Energy** | Solar arrays, exploration, pods | ⚡ |
| **Fuel** | Refineries, pods, trading | ⛽ |

Resources accumulate automatically based on your building levels. Check your rates in the dock panel.

### Buildings

Dock at your station to access the build menu. Buildings produce resources and unlock capabilities:

| Building | What It Does |
|----------|-------------|
| **Station** | Your base — its level caps all other buildings |
| **Mine** | Produces Ore per second |
| **Solar Array** | Produces Energy per second |
| **Habitat** | Produces Food per second |
| **Warehouse** | Increases resource storage cap (+500 per level) |
| **Dock** | Required for building larger ships |
| **Refinery** | Produces Fuel per second |
| **Shield** | Protects your star from raids |
| **Cannon** | Defends against enemy ships |

**Building order tip:** Upgrade your Station first — it unlocks higher levels for everything else. Then focus on Mines and Solar to fund further expansion.

### Construction

- Buildings take real time to complete (minutes to hours depending on level)
- Only one building can be under construction per star at a time
- Higher levels cost more resources and take longer

## Ships & Fleet

You start with a Scout and can work your way up to massive warships.

### Upgrade Path

```
Scout → Destroyer → Frigate → Battleship → Command Cruiser → Dreadnought
```

Each upgrade consumes the previous ship and takes time to build. You need a sufficiently leveled Dock to build larger ships.

### Special Ships

| Ship | Role | How to Get |
|------|------|-----------|
| **Basic Probe** | Reveals a nearby star without visiting | Build at dock |
| **Enhanced Probe** | Reveals a distant star with more detail | Build at dock |
| **Colony Ship** | Colonize an unclaimed star | Build at dock |
| **Freighter** | Automated resource transport between stars | Build at dock |

### Fleet Management

Once you have multiple stars, you can:
- **Transfer ships** between your stars (costs fuel, takes travel time)
- **Set freighter routes** for automated resource hauling
- **Deploy raid routes** to attack other players' systems

Access fleet management from the Fleet panel (visible in Galaxy view or when docked).

## Exploration & Discovery

### Star Discovery Levels

Stars progress through discovery states:

1. **Unexplored** — You know nothing about it
2. **Probed** — A probe has revealed basic info (resource potential, bodies)
3. **Visited** — You've physically traveled there and can see everything

### Planet Exploration

When you visit a planet for the first time, you can **Explore** it. This is a one-time roll that might yield:
- Bonus resources (ore, food, energy, fuel)
- Rare artifacts
- Anomalies with special effects

### Probing Strategy

Probes are cheap and reveal stars without risking your ship:
- **Basic Probe** → reveals the star and its planets
- **Enhanced Probe** → reveals more detail, better discovery bonuses

Send probes to plan your expansion path before committing a Colony Ship.

## Colonization — Expanding Your Empire

To claim a new star:

1. **Build a Colony Ship** at your home star's dock
2. **Transfer it** to the target unclaimed star (or fly there in Galaxy view)
3. **Dock** at the target star's station
4. Press **COLONIZE**

The colony ship is consumed and the star becomes yours. You'll get a fresh set of buildings to develop. Each new star is an independent economy — build it up just like your home star.

**Tip:** Probe stars first to find ones with good exploration bonuses before committing a Colony Ship.

## Multiplayer & Social

### Other Players

You'll see other players as ghost ships flying around. Their names appear above their ships. Everyone shares the same galaxy.

### Alliances

Join or create an alliance to:
- Share an alliance chat channel
- See allied fleet positions
- Coordinate expansion and defense

Invites appear in your Communications panel.

### Communications

Access the **Coms** panel to:
- Send **Direct Messages** to other players you've encountered
- Chat in **Public** channels
- Talk in your **Alliance** chat
- Issue **Fleet Commands** to coordinate group actions

### Trading

Some stars have **Trade Stations** where you can buy and sell resources at dynamic exchange rates. Useful for converting surplus resources into what you need.

---

## Combat & Defense

### Shooting

Press **Space** to fire your weapons. Ships auto-aim at the nearest target. Your weapon power depends on your ship class — bigger ships hit harder.

### Raids

Set a **Raid Route** to send ships to attack another player's star. They'll engage the defender's ships and potentially steal resources.

### Defense

- **Shield** buildings protect your star from incoming raids
- **Cannon** buildings automatically fire at enemy ships
- Higher-level defenses are harder to overcome

## Tips for Having Fun

### Early Game (First Session)
- Focus on one star — build Mine, Solar, Hab to get resources flowing
- Upgrade your Station to level 2-3 to unlock more buildings
- Explore every planet in your home system for bonus resources
- Collect fuel pods in asteroid belts — fuel is precious early on

### Mid Game (Days 2-5)
- Build probes and scout nearby stars for colonization targets
- Upgrade your ship along the path (Destroyer → Frigate → etc.)
- Build a Warehouse to increase storage caps
- Start a second colony at a rich star

### Late Game (Week+)
- Manage multiple colonies with freighter routes
- Join or form an alliance for coordination
- Build Dreadnoughts for fleet dominance
- Compete on the weekly leaderboard

### General Tips

- **Dock frequently** — your station is where you manage everything
- **Check back often** — buildings complete in real time, resources accumulate while you're away
- **Use the Galaxy view** — zoom out to see the big picture and plan jumps
- **Talk to people** — the Coms panel lets you coordinate, trade info, or just chat
- **Send probes first** — never colonize blind, always probe first
