# Cannon Turret Sprite Prompts

## Style: Hand-Painted Illustrated

### Restyle Prompt (all levels)

Restyle this cannon turret into a hand-painted illustrated space station component. 
We want o emphsize the hand painted aspect with a touch of cartoon, its not meant to be realistic.
Replace the dark gunmetal and blue neon glow with warm silver-gray metal, copper and red accent panels, and soft orange-amber lighting. Add visible paint texture and slight weathering marks. Keep the same cannon shape and angle but make it look like a hand-drawn game sprite rather than a 3D render. Maintain the transparent background.

### Specs

- Output: 256×256 PNG, RGBA (transparent background), sRGB
- Levels: 1–8 (cannon_lv1_256.png through cannon_lv8_256.png)
- Tool: ZenCreator (upload reference → restyle)
- Post-processing: process_cannons.py (remove white bg, crop, pad to square, resize)
